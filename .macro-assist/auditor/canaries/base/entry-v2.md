## H-101 — In a stressed S&P tape, the OR state widens Gold's forward distribution on both sides {: #h-101 }

**Status:** `draft` · drafted 2026-09-25 · explore look 2026-09-26 · prepared for promotion 2026-09-27; needs an independent audit before it is a hypothesis (§6, ADR-0022).

**What was seen.** Nothing yet specific to this entry. The question comes from [KB-016]'s precision trade: the OR flag fires on many days that are not followed by an S&P drawdown, and nobody has asked what the *other* assets do on those days.

**Where.** `explore_conditioner.py` arms `dd_bin` and `dd_x_frag`, which already quote Gold, conditioned on the S&P's drawdown bin and on the S&P drawdown bin × `fragility_or` state. Report: `results/explore_conditioner/report.md`, once the look has run.

**Mechanism it would imply.** In a stressed S&P tape (drawdown ≤ −5%), Gold faces two opposite flows: a haven bid, and forced selling to meet margin calls elsewhere. When the tape is also structurally fragile (the OR flag fires: the fragility composite, the absorption ratio or turbulence is at its own point-in-time top decile), both flows are stronger at once. Gold's outcomes should spread in *both* directions, by more than Gold's own volatility accounts for. The flag should say nothing about which way Gold goes.

**The prediction that is not the score.** Within the stressed bin, the OR-Elevated cell's P25–P75 width is at least 1.2 times the Normal cell's at every horizon (5, 10, 20 days), and the ratio does not shrink from 5d to 20d. The widening is two-sided: the Elevated cell's P25 sits below the Normal cell's *and* its P75 sits above it, at every horizon. Two-sidedness alone does not separate the mechanism from the confound below, because any rise in Gold's volatility widens both tails. So **the mechanism clause** is the contrast vol cannot produce by accident: divide each day's forward change by a σ known on that day, and the Elevated cell is still at least 1.15 times as wide as the Normal cell at every horizon, under both of the two σs named below. If the fragile tape were only a high-vol week for Gold, the σ would already carry the spread and that ratio would sit near 1.

**The confound.** Gold's own volatility clustering. A fragile S&P tape may simply be a high-vol week for Gold as well, and the width split would then be a vol forecast restated ([KB-033]). The rival is `har_scaled` on Gold, and the mechanism clause is the test against it: the first σ is `har_scaled`'s forecast for that day and horizon. Its weak point, named now: `har_scaled` is built from past returns and lags a jump in Gold's vol, so a state that fires at the start of stress spells could clear the ratio on that lag alone. The second σ is Gold's trailing 10-day realized σ, scaled to the horizon, which catches a jump within days; the clause must hold under both. Secondary: the stressed bin is small, so its episodes are counted as well as its rows.

**What would test it.** One counted explore look first, with its rival in the same run: Gold's realized forward change by S&P drawdown bin × OR state, widths and P25/P75 at 5, 10 and 20 days, with distinct stress spells counted per cell; and the same stressed cells on forward changes divided by each of the two σs. If the raw split holds and both standardized ratios clear 1.15, the state carries something the vol forecast does not. Then, only if both hold, a shadow conditioner under WP-23.B's class bar: pinball loss on Gold's P25/P50/P75 against `unconditional`, and against `har_scaled` as the second comparator, with the standardized width ratio as the mechanism clause. The class bar scores all three quantiles, so a read could clear it on the median alone; a read that clears the bar while the mechanism clause fails on the sealed slice is `unexplained`, not `edge`. **Underpowered floor**, fixed now, before any cell size is known: a sealed cell with fewer than 150 rows or fewer than 8 distinct stress spells makes the read `underpowered`. **Slice:** the whole sealed side, from `SEAL_START` 2018-01-01 (`resolved.md` #19). **One read**, with the result going to the Knowledge Base either way.

**Target-space check.** A width, and a two-sided one: a property of the conditional distribution, read by the distribution scorer. No location claim. Passes.

What the run showed, stressed bin (S&P drawdown ≤ −5%), OR-Elevated vs Normal:
- Width (P75−P25): 3.33 vs 2.54 at 5d (ratio 1.31) · 4.83 vs 3.48 at 10d (1.39) · 6.67 vs 4.39 at 20d (1.52). At least 1.2 at every horizon, and growing: the pre-registered prediction held.
- Two-sided at every horizon: Elevated P25 below Normal's (−1.62 vs −1.18 · −2.31 vs −1.62 · −3.05 vs −2.24) and P75 above it (1.71 vs 1.36 · 2.52 vs 1.86 · 3.62 vs 2.15).
- Cell sizes: 169 and 286 rows (11 and 27 distinct stress spells), above the floor of 150 rows and 8 spells.
- Mechanism clause, widths in σ units: divided by `har_scaled`'s forecast, 1.63 vs 1.34 at 5d (1.22) · 1.67 vs 1.33 at 10d (1.26) · 1.74 vs 1.35 at 20d (1.29); divided by Gold's trailing 10-day realized σ, 1.55 vs 1.31 (1.18) · 1.58 vs 1.32 (1.20) · 1.63 vs 1.32 (1.23). Both clear 1.15 at every horizon, on the same 169 and 286 rows. The split shrinks once vol is divided out, from 1.31–1.52 to 1.18–1.29: vol carries part of it, and the rest is what the state is claimed to add.
- Skill of the two arms read, Gold, against `unconditional` (report § *Per-asset skill*): `dd_x_frag` +0.0041, +0.0036 and +0.0027 at 5, 10 and 20 days, `dd_bin` +0.0012, −0.0009 and −0.0031, every 95% interval spanning zero, all far under the class bar's `MIN_SKILL = 0.02`. That is the honest prior for the sealed read, and it is low: the claim concerns 169 of the 1893 report dates, so even a real width effect moves an all-dates score very little. Whether a read with that prior is worth spending is the owner's call, not this entry's.

Not claimed: anything about Gold's median, any other asset, or any horizon beyond 20 days.
